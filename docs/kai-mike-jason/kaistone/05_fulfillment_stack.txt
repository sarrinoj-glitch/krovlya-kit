Kai Stone’s fulfillment and delivery model is built on **"systems, not 
services,"** utilizing white-labeled GoHighLevel (GHL) software to provide 
automated outcomes for local businesses at a low-ticket recurring price point 
[1-3].

### **1. GoHighLevel SaaS Setup and White-Labeling**
*   **The Model:** Stone white-labels GHL, reselling its multi-billion dollar 
infrastructure as his own proprietary system (Stone Systems) [2, 4, 5].
*   **Pricing & Rebilling:** The core offer is positioned at **$297 per month** 
[1, 6, 7]. While early iterations used tiers (e.g., $97, $197), he now largely 
pushes a single **$297 plan** to ensure clients receive the full suite of 
automations, which he claims provides the best results [7-10].
*   **The White-Labeled App:** The system is delivered through the **Lead 
Connector mobile app** [11-13]. He "forces" clients to use this app for their 
daily communication, making the service "sticky" as it becomes the business's 
central command center for leads, reviews, and calendars [14-16].

### **2. Website Building Process and Turnaround**
*   **The Turnaround:** The standard delivery time for a new website is **7 to 
10 days** (most frequently cited as **9 days**) after the client submits their 
onboarding information [17-21].
*   **The Process:** 
    *   **Templatization:** Stone uses **one core website template** (15 to 25 
pages) for an entire niche (e.g., all plumbers get the same layout) [22-25]. 
    *   **Custom Values:** GHL **"Snapshots"** are used to copy the system 
structure into a new account [26-28]. The specific business data is populated 
through **"custom values"** (e.g., phone number, email, service areas) that 
automatically update across the entire site once inputted in the backend 
[29-32].
    *   **AI Implementation:** He uses **ChatGPT** to generate SEO-optimized 
copy from the client's onboarding form and recently began using GHL’s **AI 
Website Builder** to create demo sites in under 5 minutes [29, 33-35].

### **3. AI Systems and Automations Delivered**
The "System" consists of four to five core features designed to capture and 
convert leads:
*   **Auto Call Text Back:** An automated text sent instantly if a business 
owner misses a phone call (e.g., "Sorry I missed you, how can I help?") [36-38].
*   **AI Conversation/Text Bot:** A bot that follows up with leads from the 
website, social media, or missed calls via text to book appointments [39-42].
*   **Five-Star Google Review Funnel:** A "review gating" system. If a customer 
selects 4 or 5 stars, they are sent to the public Google page; 1-3 stars are 
diverted to a "private feedback" form to prevent public bad reviews [43-47].
*   **AI Voice Receptionist:** An advanced agent that answers calls and books 
appointments live on the phone [48-50].
*   **Instant Lead Follow-up:** A chat widget or quote form on the website that 
initiates an immediate text conversation with new leads rather than sending them
to email [36, 51, 52].

### **4. Client Onboarding and Success**
*   **The Form:** Immediately after payment, the client receives a **detailed 
onboarding form** [53-55]. Stone tells them this is the most important step: 
"The better you do on this onboarding form, the better your website's going to 
be" [29, 53].
*   **The Launch/Onboarding Call:** Once the site is built (approx. 9 days), the
client has a call with a **Customer Success Manager (CSM)** [54, 56-58]. The CSM
walks the client through the system, shows them how to use the mobile app, and 
makes final photo/wording tweaks [18, 59, 60].
*   **Outsourcing Delivery:** Stone does not press buttons. He hires 
**project-based Virtual Assistants (VAs)** (usually from the Philippines via 
Onlinejobs.ph or Upwork) and pays them **$30 to $60** to set up the entire 
client account and website using his SOPs [61-65].

### **5. Retention and Churn-Reduction Tactics**
*   **Database Reactivation (The "Quick Win"):** To prevent early churn, Stone 
offers an immediate text blast to the client's old lead list to generate instant
reviews or jobs (e.g., a "10% off Christmas special") [66-69].
*   **Sticking Point:** By integrating their **calendars, invoicing, and 
messaging** into the white-labeled app, the business becomes reliant on the 
software to function, making it difficult to cancel [11, 14, 15, 70].
*   **The Buyout Fee:** If a client wishes to leave but keep the website he 
built, Stone charges a **$1,000 to $1,300 buyout fee** to recoup acquisition 
costs [71, 72].
*   **Stated Churn:** Churn rate is cited between **7% and 8.3%** [73, 74].
