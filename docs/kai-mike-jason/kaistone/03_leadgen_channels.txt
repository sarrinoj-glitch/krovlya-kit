Beyond cold calling, Kai Stone utilizes and recommends several acquisition 
channels, shifting from manual, one-to-one outreach to scalable "one-to-many" 
systems as revenue grows [1, 2]. He specifically emphasizes that while cold 
calling builds the necessary sales skills, paid ads and automation are the only 
ways to achieve "hyperscale" [3-5].

### **1. Meta (Facebook & Instagram) Ads**
Stone identifies Meta ads as the fastest way to scale any company and recommends
starting them once an agency reaches **$3,000 to $5,000 in monthly recurring 
revenue (MRR)** [6, 7].
*   **The Funnel Role:** He uses a three-stage evolution for ad funnels:
    *   **Version 1 (Flow/Scrappy):** Meta Lead Form ads where users stay on 
Facebook and provide only a name and number [8, 9]. This prioritizes "flow" over
"friction" to get high volume [10]. Leads here often cost **$10 to $20 each** 
but are typically lower quality [11, 12].
    *   **Version 2 (Friction/Quality):** Ads that direct users to a landing 
page with qualifying questions (e.g., current revenue, business ownership) [8, 
11, 13].
    *   **Version 3 (Hyperscale):** A "Book-a-Call" funnel where the landing 
page includes a 5-10 minute **Video Sales Letter (VSL)** that pre-sells the 
prospect before they book a meeting on a calendar [4, 14].
*   **The "Local Hero" Strategy:** For beginners with a small budget, he 
recommends filming ads in front of local landmarks (e.g., the Austin Bridge) to 
build instant trust with local business owners [15, 16].
*   **Numbers:** He currently spends **$10,000 to $15,000 per month** on ads and
cites an average front-end acquisition cost of **$300 per client** [5, 17-19].

### **2. Cold SMS**
Stone recommends cold SMS as the **number one outreach method** for beginners 
who do not have money for paid ads [20].
*   **The Funnel Role:** It functions as a scalable version of cold calling 
because it is "one-to-many," allowing an agency to reach hundreds of prospects 
per day rather than dozens [2]. 
*   **Strategy:** He suggests testing different scripts to see what resonates 
and then driving interested responses to a landing page or booking link [21, 
22].
*   **Numbers:** His business partner, Mike, reportedly scaled to 
**$8,000–$9,000 MRR** using strictly cold SMS [7].

### **3. Content and YouTube (Pre-Selling)**
Stone views his YouTube channel and company content as a massive "authority" 
builder that reduces sales resistance [23-25].
*   **The Funnel Role:** Content serves to **pre-sell** prospects so they arrive
at sales calls already knowing the price point ($297/mo) and the product's value
[26-28]. 
*   **The 6-Minute Pre-Call Video:** Stone sends a specific "pre-all" video to 
every prospect. If they haven't watched it, he often refuses to take the call or
makes them watch it on the spot [26, 28, 29]. This ensures they "speak the same 
language" regarding features like the "five-star review funnel" [26, 30].
*   **Numbers:** His YouTube channel alone generates approximately **$40,000 to 
$50,000 per month** in recurring revenue [31].

### **4. Cold Email and Loom Videos**
This is a high-personalization strategy Stone recommends for those who are 
comfortable on camera but lack an ad budget [32].
*   **The Funnel Role:** Outreach starts with a cold email. If a prospect 
responds positively, Stone sends a **30-45 second personalized Loom video** 
[32].
*   **Strategy:** In the video, he shares his screen, pulls up the prospect's 
current "trash" website, and picks it apart to show exactly how his system would
fix it [32-34]. 

### **5. Referrals and Partnerships**
Stone leverages referrals and strategic outsourcing to handle services his 
agency does not internally fulfill [17, 35].
*   **The Funnel Role:** Referrals build long-term stability; he notes that for

[36].
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** Because Stone avoids "nightmare" tasks like

also refers out Meta ad management for contractors to specialized partners like 
"Corey" [17].

### **6. Manual "Swamp" Methods (Walk-ins and DMs)**
For the first **1 to 3 clients**, Stone suggests "scrappy" methods that require 
no software [20, 39].
*   **Walk-ins:** He recommends walking into local restaurants, food trucks, or 
med spas and offering to build a website for free in exchange for a testimonial 
[39-41].
*   **Cold DMs:** While not his primary method, he notes that other successful 
agency owners use **Facebook Cold DMs** to reach **$20,000 MRR** [7].
*   **Numbers:** Stone's first three clients were a dog groomer (cold call), a 
food truck, and a restaurant [39]. He notes it can cost as little as **$5 to 
$20** to hire a VA to scrape **200–400 leads** from Google Maps to fuel these 
manual efforts [42, 43].
