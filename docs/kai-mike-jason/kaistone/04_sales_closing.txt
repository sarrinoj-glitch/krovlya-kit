Kai Stone utilizes a highly streamlined, **low-ticket sales process** designed 
for volume and simplicity, typically closing deals in **15 to 25 minutes** [1, 
2].

### **1. The Pre-Call Filter**
The most critical part of Stone's process happens before the meeting even 
starts.
*   **The 6-Minute VSL:** Every prospect is sent a short video (Video Sales 
Letter) explaining exactly what the service is, how it works, and the price 
point ($297/mo) [3-5].
*   **The Filter Question:** He opens every call with: **"Did you get a chance 
to watch that short video I sent over to you?"** [3, 5, 6].
*   **The Rule:** If they haven't watched it, he either makes them watch it on 
the spot or considers the call "cooked" because they aren't "speaking the same 
language" [3, 7, 8].

### **2. Discovery and Demo Structure**
Stone avoids hour-long pitches and instead focuses on making the prospect 
**"problem aware"** [9-11].
*   **The Opening Hammer:** "Awesome, well that video is exactly what we do. 
**What questions do you have for me?**" [7, 12-14].
*   **The Google Audit:** He often Googles the prospect’s business live on 
screen. If they don't pop up or have no reviews, he uses silence to let the pain
sink in: **"I can't find you. Imagine I'm a customer... this is an easy fix, 
man"** [10, 15, 16].
*   **Outcome Over Features:** He refuses to talk about "nerdy" technical 
details like H1 tags [17]. Instead, he positions the site as a **"Command 
Center"** that ensures they never miss a lead [18, 19].
*   **Throwing Rocks at Ads:** He builds trust by telling contractors *not* to 
run $1,500/mo ads yet because their current "trash" website won't convert them 
[20-22]. He calls our system "Step 1 and 2" and ads "Step 3" [22, 23].

### **3. Offer Presentation and Pricing**
Stone presents the offer as a "no-brainer" foundation for any local business.
*   **The Core Price:** **$297 per month** [12, 24, 25].
*   **Pricing Model:** **$0 setup fee** and **no contracts** ("You can cancel 
anytime") [26-28].
*   **Cash-Forward Upsell:** He offers to optimize their Google My Business 
profile (usually a $300–$500 fee) for **free** if they pay for **three months 
upfront ($891)** [29-31].

### **4. Closing Techniques and Exact Lines**
Stone uses **"Assuming the Close,"** acting as if the prospect has already 
decided to buy [32-34].
*   **Permission to Close:** Once the prospect stops asking questions, Stone 
asks: **"Do you have any other questions before I get you going?"** [35-37].
*   **The Commitment Line:** **"Do you have photos of your work that you can 
send us for the website?"** [34, 38, 39]. By agreeing to send photos, they have 
mentally committed to the project [33, 40].
*   **The Card-Last Strategy:** He shares his screen and fills out their 
business name and billing address first to build momentum. He **never asks** 
"Are you ready to pay?" but instead says: **"And what is the card number over 
there?"** [41-44].

### **5. Handling Objections on the Call**
*   **"When will I get leads?":** "I can't prove that Susan cut you a $50,000

professional than your competitors" [45-47].
*   **"It seems too good to be true":** He levels with them as a business owner:
"When you have 1,300 clients paying $300, it's a snowball effect. I’m just being
honest" [48, 49].
*   **"I need to talk to my wife/fiancé":** He is often "douchey" or blunt here:
**"It's a yes or no situation. We don't do follow-up calls... I don't think your
fiancé is going to change the math"** [50, 51].

### **6. Follow-up Cadence and Metrics**
*   **Follow-up Philosophy:** Stone is **anti-follow-up**. He believes if you 
don't close on the first call, the odds drop to near zero [51]. He tells 
prospects, "I hate telemarketing, I don't want to keep bothering you" [52, 53].
*   **Close Rate:** He states he closes between **1 in 2 and 1 in 3** prospects

*   **Show Rate:** Approximately **1 in 4** booked meetings actually show up

*   **Average Deal Size:** His Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) ranges from
